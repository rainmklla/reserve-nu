Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a room reservation?

+

Reservations are made through the ReserveNU portal. Log in with your school credentials, navigate to the 'Rooms' section, select your desired date and time, and submit the purpose of your reservation. You will receive an email confirmation once approved.

What types of rooms are available for booking?

+

We offer various types of rooms including standard classrooms, conference halls, computer laboratories, and specialized studio spaces. Each room listing provides details on capacity and available equipment.

Is there a time limit for reservations?

+

Standard reservations are limited to a maximum of four hours. For longer events, please submit a special request form available on the main dashboard, which requires approval from the Facilities Department.

Can I cancel or modify an existing reservation?

+

Yes, you can cancel a reservation up to 24 hours before the scheduled time directly through your user dashboard. Modifications (like changing time or date) require canceling the original booking and creating a new one.

What should I do if the room is already occupied?

+

If you arrive at your reserved room and find it occupied, please politely show the occupants your confirmation email. If the issue persists, contact campus security or the administrative office immediately.

Do I need special permission to book a large hall?

+

Reservations for halls with a capacity over 50 require pre-approval from the Student Affairs office, which you can request directly within the reservation form before submitting your final booking.
